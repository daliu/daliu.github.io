Read the sample, not a verdict.

Unique browsers are GA4 totalUsers across the whole report window, not a sum of daily users and not a count of people or IP addresses. Cookies, multiple devices, blocking and opt-in participation affect the estimate. Only online events after opting in are included. QA events are excluded. The total browser count covers all modes/difficulties and does not change with the filters below it.

Death rate uses resolved attempts: deaths ÷ (deaths + clears). Abandoned fights are listed separately. Strategy counts describe the most common weapon or a detected combo/movement habit in an encounter; they do not prove that a strategy caused a win. Community reports contain aggregate gameplay counts, never individual histories or browser identifiers.
